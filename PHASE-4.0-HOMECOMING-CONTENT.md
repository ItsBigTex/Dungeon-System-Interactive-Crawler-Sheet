## Firearm design
No new firearm definition is automatically awarded by this pack. The gun-store content is designed around firearm-adjacent utility gear and scarce resources so crawlers' starting mundane firearms remain useful without turning Floor One into an unlimited gun upgrade path.

## RAW guardrails used
- First-Floor ordinary Mobs are not a gold-farming source.
- Tutorial-floor progression emphasizes gear, loot, achievements, and boxes.
- Neighborhood Boss defeat can award the appropriate map reward.
- Campaign-specific effects remain explicitly THE_DESCENT content.
